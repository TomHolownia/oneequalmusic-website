      <p>We hold general auditions for our three ensembles: One Equal Music, OEM Emerging Artists and OEM Foundation. You do one audition, and the Directors place you in the ensemble that suits you best.</p>
      <p>The requirements below are guidelines, not hard rules. We want to grow enthusiastic choral singers and build their skills with them. Every role is paid per call.</p>
      <p>If you, or someone you know, would like to do more choral singing, please get in touch.</p>

  - type: text
    eyebrow: How to apply
    heading: Applying and the audition
    body: |-
      <p>Email <a href="mailto:admin@oneequalmusic.org?subject=Audition">admin@oneequalmusic.org</a> with your CV and the following:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Your name and the city you are based in</li>
        <li>Your voice type</li>
        <li>The choirs and ensembles you have sung with</li>
        <li>Any music degree, and your vocal training (years of lessons and your teachers)</li>
        <li>Any instruments you play</li>
        <li>Which ensembles you are most interested in</li>
      </ul>
      <p>After an initial screening, you may be invited to a live audition with the Co-Directors. It includes:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>A short chat about your CV, how you heard about OEM and why you would like to sing with us</li>
        <li>A solo piece of your choice that shows the range you are most comfortable in (accompaniment provided if you like)</li>
        <li>A range check, if needed</li>
        <li>Sight-singing: a piece sent to you a week before, plus an early work and a modern work</li>
      </ul>
