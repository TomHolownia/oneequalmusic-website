      <p>One Equal Music (OEM) is Brisbane’s professional choral ensemble. Our repertoire spans from some of the earliest documented vocal music through to music written for the ensemble by living composers. The principal singers in OEM come from the leading Cathedral and Parish choirs in Queensland, and are selected for their musicianship and trained voices.</p>
      <p>Our name comes from the poem <em>Bring Us, O Lord God</em> by John Donne, set to music by William Henry Harris. OEM strives to establish equality for all musicians involved in the ensemble.</p>
      <p>OEM was established in 2022 and is co-directed by husband-and-wife duo Tomasz Holownia and Eleanor Adeney, who have both worked extensively as professional choral singers in Australia and the UK.</p>
